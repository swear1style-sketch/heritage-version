The Shift

India is moving from outsourcing to ownership.

The India conversation has changed. It is no longer only about lower-cost delivery — it is about where global companies choose to build product, engineering, AI and R&D capability they intend to keep.

From vendor to capability

Work that used to be handed off is increasingly owned end-to-end by teams sitting inside India, reporting into the same roadmap as headquarters.

From metros to Tier-II

Talent, cost structure and quality of life are pulling capability build-out beyond Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad into cities built for the next decade, not the last one.

From project to centre

The destination for a serious engineering relationship is increasingly a dedicated centre — an ODC or a GCC — not an open-ended staffing arrangement.

One India Journey

Multiple starting points, one direction of travel.

Wherever you are today — needing ten engineers next quarter, or evaluating a 300-person capability centre in three years — the journey runs through the same stages.

01
Engineering Pod
A focused team against a defined scope. The fastest way to prove out India as an option.
02
ODC
A dedicated, named engineering centre operating under your standards, without the overhead of a legal entity.
03
GCC
A fully operational Global Capability Centre — your own entity, your own team, strategic functions beyond engineering.
04
Strategic Centre
A mature, multi-function India capability that is core infrastructure to the business, not a cost line.

Illustrative stages. Not every company needs to progress through all four — Edish will help you identify the right entry point.
