FAQ

Questions buyers ask about ODCs.

How is an ODC different from staff augmentation?

+

Staff augmentation adds individuals to your existing process. An ODC is a dedicated, named team with its own identity, continuity and delivery governance — closer to an owned centre.

Can an ODC become a GCC later?

+

Yes — this is one of the most common paths. The team and institutional knowledge transfer forward rather than starting over.

Do we need a legal entity in India for an ODC?

+

No — that's the point of the model. Edish holds the entity and employment relationship while the team operates as your dedicated centre.

How big can an ODC get?

+

There's no fixed ceiling, but centres that grow past a certain size and strategic scope often benefit from converting to a GCC for full ownership and control.
