Build Your India GCC With a Partner That Can Start Before the GCC Exists.

A Global Capability Centre is the strategic destination for most serious India relationships. Edish works across the full arc — assess, design, locate, structure, build, launch and scale — so the same partner who helps you decide can also help you deliver.

Context

A GCC decision is bigger than a hiring decision.

A GCC touches entity structure, real estate, governance, talent strategy and multi-year cost planning. Companies that treat it as an oversized staffing exercise tend to under-invest in the decisions that matter most in year two and three.

What Edish Does

The full arc, held by one team.

Assess & design

Feasibility, function selection, operating model and business case — before any commitment to a location or a legal entity.

Locate & structure

City evaluation, entity structuring guidance (via qualified legal partners) and governance design.

Build & launch

Programme management, talent acquisition, infrastructure and the operational build-out of the centre.

Scale & transform

New functions, deeper engineering maturity, and the operating-model evolution a GCC goes through as it matures.

FAQ

Questions we're asked most.

How is a GCC different from outsourcing?

+

A GCC is your own operation — your entity, your team, your IP — built to be a permanent part of the business, not a vendor relationship you can exit at will.

Do we need to commit to a full GCC on day one?

+

No. Many companies start with an ODC or an engineering pod and convert to a GCC once the India thesis is proven.

How long does a GCC take to launch?

+

It depends heavily on function, city and entity structure. Edish will give you a realistic, case-specific timeline during the advisory phase rather than a generic promise.

Can Edish handle legal entity setup?

+

Edish coordinates with qualified legal, tax and compliance partners for anything requiring licensed professional advice, and manages that coordination on your behalf.

What if we only need engineers right now?

+

That's a valid and common starting point. See Engineering and ODC — both connect naturally into a GCC path later if you choose.
