GCC, ODC, BOT or Managed Model? Choose the Right Starting Point.

Four common ways to start an India capability, compared on ownership, speed, investment and where each one tends to lead.

There is no universal winner — the right model depends on how much control, speed and investment fit your situation today.

Decision Framework

A simple way to think about it.

Need capacity fast, low commitment?

Staff augmentation or a Dedicated Team is the practical entry point.

Want a named, dedicated centre without a legal entity?

An ODC gives you control and identity without incorporation overhead.

Know you want full ownership eventually?

BOT lets you start vendor-operated and transfer to full ownership on a defined timeline.

Ready to commit to India as core infrastructure?

A GCC is the destination — full ownership, full strategic scope.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask when comparing models.

Can we start with one model and move to another?

+

Yes — this is the most common path. Staff augmentation or ODC into GCC is a well-worn route, not an exception.

Is BOT right for every company?

+

No. BOT suits companies that know they want eventual ownership but aren't ready to build the entity and governance themselves from day one.

What does 'Managed GCC' mean exactly?

+

You own the entity and the team; Edish provides ongoing operational management — delivery governance, HR operations, facilities coordination — so you don't have to run every function of the centre yourself.
