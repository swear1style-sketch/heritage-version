Why India

India Is Becoming a Global Capability Platform.

The case for India has shifted from cost arbitrage to genuine capability — engineering, product ownership, AI and R&D that global companies choose to keep, not just outsource.

The Evolution

From delivery centre to capability platform.

Engineering & R&D ownership

India teams increasingly own roadmap and architecture decisions, not just execution against someone else's spec.

AI & digital capability

A deep, fast-growing pool of AI and data engineering talent, concentrated in both metro and emerging Tier-II hubs.

Talent & operating model maturity

A generation of engineering leaders who have run global-standard delivery organisations, not just teams.

Economics & risk

A cost structure that remains favourable even as compensation rises, provided the operating model is designed well.
